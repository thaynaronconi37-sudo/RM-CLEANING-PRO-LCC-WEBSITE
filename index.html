Services

What we offer for homeowners like you

Regular Cleaning

Our recurring services, available weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly, ensure your living spaces, bedrooms, bathrooms, and kitchen remain spotless with safe, high-quality cleaning agents.

Deep Cleaning

When regular cleaning isn’t enough, our deep cleaning service provides a thorough reset, focusing on neglected areas. We use pet-safe, non-toxic products to eliminate allergens and restore freshness.

Move-In/Out Cleaning

Our move-in and move-out cleaning services guarantee that your new home or rental is immaculate. We sanitize cabinets and detail appliances, allowing you to focus on your move without stress.

Commercial Cleaning

We offer after-hours commercial cleaning for small offices and retail spaces in Greater Pittsburgh, sanitizing workstations and maintaining breakrooms and restrooms without disrupting your operations.

Post-Construction Cleaning

We remove drywall dust from walls, fixtures, and trim, scrub floors, clean window tracks, and polish surfaces, readying newly renovated spaces for immediate use.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is included in a standard house cleaning vs. a deep cleaning?

A standard cleaning covers routine maintenance such as dusting, vacuuming, mopping, bathroom sanitization, and kitchen surface cleaning. A deep cleaning targets accumulated dust and grime, including hand-washing baseboards, scrubbing tile grout, cleaning inside appliances, and detailing doors and trim.

Are your cleaning products safe for dogs and cats?

Yes. We use high-quality, non-toxic products that effectively sanitize surfaces without leaving residues or fumes that could harm pets or children.

What languages does your cleaning staff speak?

We offer full trilingual customer support and service in English, Spanish, and Portuguese, making communication simple and direct.

  • English
  • Español
  • Português
Do I need to be home while you clean?

No. Many clients provide us with key codes or garage access codes. Our team operates Monday through Friday between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM, maintaining strict security and respect for your property whether you are home or away.
